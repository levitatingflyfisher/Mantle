Discover the aesthetic from revealed preference, then merge in a way that rewards agreement — not enthusiasm. A shared favourite is one you BOTH ranked high, not one person loved and the other tolerated.

• Pairwise "which is more us?" over a deck of images
• A printable House Charter: your Spine, what is Contested, and the through-lines
• Ranking maths that will not let one loud voice win

No accounts, no cloud, no ads. The APK asks for no network permission at all.
